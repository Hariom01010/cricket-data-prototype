Q1: Timing & Match Scheduling

CricketData.org provides dateTimeGMT (ISO 8601 UTC timestamp). Your OpenAPI spec specifies two lock policies: BEFORE_MATCH_START (locks at dateTimeGMT - 5m) and BEFORE_TOSS (locks at dateTimeGMT - 30m). Exact timestamps are calculated directly from this field to schedule automated publishing and submission cutoffs.
